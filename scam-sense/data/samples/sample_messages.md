# Fictional Sample Messages

These examples are fictional and intended for product planning and future testing. They should not be used to contact real people, organizations, or payment accounts.

## Fake Delivery SMS

> Your parcel could not be delivered today because the address is incomplete. Pay a $2.14 redelivery fee now to avoid return: `https://delivery-review-example.test/pay`

Potential risk signals:

- Urgent delivery pressure
- Small unexpected fee
- Link requesting payment
- Vague sender identity

## Fake Bank Alert

> SECURITY ALERT: Your account has been temporarily locked due to suspicious activity. Confirm your login and one-time code immediately at `https://secure-bank-update-example.test` or your card will be suspended.

Potential risk signals:

- Account-lock threat
- Request for login details or one-time code
- Urgent deadline
- Link that may imitate a bank

## Marketplace Buyer Scam

> Hi, I want to buy the camera today. I will send a courier, but the courier needs you to pay a refundable insurance fee first. Please use this payment link so the pickup can be confirmed.

Potential risk signals:

- Unusual courier arrangement
- Seller asked to pay a fee
- Pressure to complete quickly
- Payment link outside the marketplace

## Crypto Investment DM

> I help new investors earn guaranteed daily returns from crypto trading. Send $300 today and I will turn it into $3,000 by next week. Limited spots, message me your wallet details now.

Potential risk signals:

- Guaranteed returns
- Large promised profit
- Urgency and scarcity
- Request for wallet details or funds

## Fake Job Offer

> Congratulations, you have been selected for a remote assistant role paying $95 per hour. No interview is required. To activate payroll, purchase three gift cards and send photos of the codes for verification.

Potential risk signals:

- Unrealistic pay
- No interview
- Gift card request
- Payroll or verification excuse

## Urgent Family Emergency Scam

> Mum, I dropped my phone and this is my temporary number. I am in trouble and need you to send $1,800 right now. Please do not call, just transfer it quickly and I will explain later.

Potential risk signals:

- Impersonation of a family member
- Urgent emotional pressure
- Request not to call
- Immediate money transfer request

## Rental Deposit Scam

> The apartment is available, but I am overseas. Send the bond today before viewing to reserve the property.

Potential risk signals:

- Deposit requested before viewing
- Overseas-owner explanation
- Urgent reservation pressure
- Rental context

## Fake Tax Refund

> ATO tax refund pending. Enter your bank details at `https://mygov-refund-example.test` immediately to receive it.

Potential risk signals:

- Government impersonation context
- Bank-detail request
- Suspicious fictional link
- Urgency pressure

## Lower-Risk Routine Reminder

> Reminder: your appointment is tomorrow at 3 pm. Please open the provider's usual app if you need to make a change.

Expected behaviour:

- No obvious scam-risk indicators detected
- Still encourages use of a known official channel

## Ambiguous Account Note

> Please check your account when you have a moment.

Expected behaviour:

- Limited context and low confidence
- No link, payment, urgency, credential, or identity request
