# Australian Safety and Reporting Guidance

ScamSense offers educational risk guidance, not official, legal, financial, cybersecurity or law-enforcement advice.

## Stop and verify

- Do not click an unexpected link, open an attachment, disclose a password or verification code, or transfer money under pressure.
- Contact the person or organisation through details you find independently in its official app or website.
- For a family emergency message, call the person on a known existing number or check with another trusted relative.

## If money or information was shared

- Contact the relevant bank, card issuer or service provider immediately using its official contact details.
- Change affected passwords through the official service, especially anywhere the same password was reused.
- Follow the Australian Government's [cyber.gov.au scam recovery guidance](https://www.cyber.gov.au/report-and-recover/recover-from/scams).

## Report a scam

Use the Australian Government's [Scamwatch reporting form](https://portal.scamwatch.gov.au/report-a-scam/). A Scamwatch report helps authorities understand and disrupt scams, but it is not an official police report. Follow Scamwatch and cyber.gov.au guidance for the reporting path appropriate to the circumstances.

These links and recommendations were verified against Scamwatch and cyber.gov.au on 23 July 2026.
